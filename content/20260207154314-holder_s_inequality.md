Suppose \\(p \in [1, \infty]\\) and \\(1/p + 1/q = 1\\). Then \\(\forall x,y\in\mathbb{R}^{d}\\),

\\[
\left|x\cdot y\right| \leq \left|x\right|\_p \left|y \right|\_q
\\]
