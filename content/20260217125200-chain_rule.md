If \\(g\\) is [differentiable]({{< relref "20260213183357-derivatives.md" >}}) at \\(a\\) and \\(f\\) is diff. at \\(g(a)\\),
then \\(f \circ g\\) is diff. at \\(a\\) and \\((f \circ g)'(a) = f'(g(a))\cdot g'(a)\\)

<div class="proof">

We define
\\[
G(x) = \begin{cases}
$\frac{g(x)-g(a)}{x-a}$ if $x \neq a$ \\\\
$g'(a)$ if $x = a$
\end{cases}
\\]

</div>
