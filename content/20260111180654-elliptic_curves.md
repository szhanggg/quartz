## Definition {#definition}

Elliptic curves refer to the study of solutions of equations of a certain form. The actual connection to ellipses is tenuous.
The curve is a plane which consists of solutions \\((x, y)\\) for:
\\(y^2 = x^3+ax+b\\)


## Facts {#facts}

For any cubic curve of genus 1, we can construct every rational point by using chords and tangents starting from a fixed set of points.
